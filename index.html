Frequently asked questions

How do I choose the right shoe size?

Measure your foot in the evening, compare it with our size guide, and leave about 1 cm of space at the toe. If you are between sizes, choose the larger one or message our fitting team.

Do you offer exchanges on shoes?

Yes. You can exchange unworn shoes within 14 days of delivery if the size or fit is not right.

How long does delivery take?

Orders reach most cities in Pakistan within 2-4 working days. Karachi orders often arrive sooner.

Which shoes are best for running and gym?

Choose cushioned running shoes for road running, and stable sports shoes with a flatter sole for gym and cross-training.

How do I clean my leather and canvas shoes?

Wipe leather with a damp cloth and use a leather conditioner. Hand-wash canvas with mild soap and let it air dry away from direct sun.

Can I visit the store before buying?

Of course. Our shoes store is open Monday to Saturday, 10am to 8pm. See our location page for directions.

Not sure which shoes to pick?

Tell us how you plan to wear them and we will recommend a pair. Read about us or visit our store.
