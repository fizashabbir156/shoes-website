About StrideNest, your friendly shoes store

We started StrideNest because buying good shoes should be simple, honest and comfortable.

Our story

StrideNest began in 2019 as a small shop in Karachi, run by two friends who were tired of overpriced sneakers and badly fitting formal shoes. We picked a short list of makers we trusted and tested every pair ourselves.

Today our online shoes store ships across Pakistan. We keep a focused range of running shoes, sports shoes, casual sneakers, formal shoes and hiking boots, so you spend less time scrolling and more time walking.

What we believe in

Comfort first

A good shoe should feel right on day one. We choose cushioning, arch support and breathable materials over trends.

Honest prices

No fake discounts. The price you see is the fair price of a well-made pair.

Made to last

We favour durable leather, rubber and mesh so your shoes last seasons, not weeks.

Meet the team behind your shoes

Our fitting experts love talking shoes. Ask about sizing, care, or the best pair for your next trip.
